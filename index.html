Our story

Folded, tied, dipped & revealed.

Elormdiz began with a simple love for colour. Each piece is folded, twisted and tied by hand, then dipped in rich dyes and left to bloom into a pattern that can never be copied.

From crisp button-up shirts to fabrics ready for your tailor, we make wearable art for people who like to stand out, quietly or loudly.
